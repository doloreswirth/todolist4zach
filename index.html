Week 1 · Oct 19–25 Start the habit

  • Stairs: 20 min, no pack
  • Strength: 2 sets of 10
  • Saturday: 90 min walk in the trek boots

“Well begun is half done.” — Aristotle

Week 2 · Oct 26–Nov 1 Get used to the pack

  • Stairs: 25 min, 10 lb
  • Strength: 2 sets of 12
  • Saturday: 2 hours, 10 lb

“Paso a paso se va lejos.” Step by step, you go far. — Dicho

Week 3 · Nov 2–8 First real hills

  • Stairs: 25 min, 10 lb
  • Strength: 3 sets of 10
  • Saturday: 2 hours, 10 lb, Blue Hills Skyline. Steady up, careful down.

“The best way out is always through.” — Robert Frost

Week 4 · Nov 9–15 Go longer

  • Stairs: 30 min, 12 lb
  • Strength: 3 sets of 10, holding the pack
  • Saturday: 2.5 hours, 12 lb, Middlesex Fells. Eat something every hour.

“Walking is the best possible exercise.” — Thomas Jefferson

Week 5 · Nov 16–22 Recovery week

  • Stairs: 20 min, 10 lb
  • Strength: 2 sets of 10
  • Saturday: 90 min, 10 lb

“Slow and steady wins the race.” — Aesop

Week 6 · Nov 23–29 Build to 3 hours

  • Stairs: 30 min, 15 lb
  • Strength: 3 sets of 8, holding the pack
  • Saturday: 3 hours, 15 lb. Home for Thanksgiving? Join the family on Dreaded Hill.

“Sí se puede.” Yes, it can be done. — Dolores Huerta

Week 7 · Nov 30–Dec 6 Peak weekend

  • Stairs: 30 min, 15 lb
  • Strength: 3 sets of 8, holding the pack
  • Saturday: 3 hours, 15 lb. Sunday: 90 min, 10 lb. Two days in a row, like the trek.

“Going to the mountains is going home.” — John Muir

Week 8 · Dec 7–13 Taper during finals

  • Stairs: once, 20 min, 10 lb
  • Strength: 2 sets, light
  • Saturday: 1 hour, easy, in boots. Rest is training now.

“To travel hopefully is a better thing than to arrive.” — Robert Louis Stevenson

Strength · 20 min

  • Squats
  • Step-ups on a chair or stair
  • Reverse lunges
  • Single-leg deadlifts
  • Plank, 30–45 sec

Boston trails

  • Blue Hills: Great Blue Hill via Skyline
  • Middlesex Fells: Skyline Outer Reservoir Loop

Rules

  • Every long walk in the trek boots.
  • Walk down the stairs — descents are the hard part.
  • Icy trail? Do the long walk on city streets with the pack.
  • Missed a session? Skip it, don’t double up.
